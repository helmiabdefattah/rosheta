## Suggested launch plan (2 weeks, 3 posts a week)

Pin post **01** to the top of the page, and boost **02** (free trial) to doctors and clinic owners
in your city.

| Day | Post | Why here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 01 · Whole clinic in one place | Introduces the product — pin it |
| 3 | 03 · Reservations | The pain everyone recognises first |
| 5 | 06 · Electronic prescription | The most visible "wow" feature |
| 8 | 02 · Free trial | Converts the interest built in week one |
| 10 | 05 · Clinic + patient apps | Broad appeal, very shareable |
| 12 | 07 · Invoices and reports | Speaks to the clinic owner's wallet |
| 15 | 08 · Waiting-area screen | Visual, easy to picture in their clinic |
| 17 | 04 · Notifications | Fewer no-shows = money |
| 19 | 09 · Reception app | Speaks to the staff who will champion you |
| 22 | 10 · Medical history | Depth: this is a real medical record |
| 24 | 11 · Website + features on demand | Ends on "we build for you", drives messages |

**Which language?** If most of your audience is Egyptian, post the Arabic image with the Arabic
caption and keep the English set for the international or multilingual page version. Posting both
languages in the same caption halves the readable text before "See more", so prefer one language
per post.

**Every post should end with either** `dev.mostashfaon.com` **or** "Message us for a demo" — never
neither.
