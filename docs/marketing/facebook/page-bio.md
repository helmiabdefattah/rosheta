### Full description ("Our story" / details)

> **mostashfaOn — connecting health.**
>
> mostashfaOn runs a clinic from the front desk to the prescription and the payment, in one online
> system. No installation: the clinic works from any browser, and every member of the team — doctor,
> assistant, reception, management — has their own login and sees exactly what they need.
>
> **What you get**
> - **Reservations** — patients request a booking, the front desk confirms, moves or cancels it in
>   one click, and working hours decide which slots can be booked at all.
> - **Notifications** — confirmations, reminders and changes are pushed straight to the patient's
>   phone, so appointments are not missed.
> - **Two mobile apps** — one for the clinic team, one for patients, both on the same live data.
> - **Reception app and self check-in** — register a new patient in under a minute, take the
>   payment, and call the next name.
> - **Waiting-area screen** — a display that shows who is being seen and who is next, updated live.
> - **The doctor's workspace** — today's list, the examination screen, diagnosis and treatment plan,
>   lab and radiology requests with their results, and examination fields each doctor designs for
>   their own specialty.
> - **Medical history** — every past visit with its diagnosis, plan, prescriptions and results, plus
>   allergies and chronic conditions always in front of the doctor.
> - **Electronic prescriptions** — medicine, dose and duration, printed on the clinic letterhead or
>   sent to the patient, and kept in the file.
> - **Invoices and reports** — what is due at the end of each visit, collections day by day, a
>   patient report, and an insurance report showing what each company owes.
> - **A doctor profile website** — a public page where patients find the clinic and book online.
> - **Features on demand** — anything specific your practice needs, built for you.
>
> **Try it free:** dev.mostashfaon.com
